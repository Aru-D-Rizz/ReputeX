Privacy Policy for ReputeX

Effective Date: August 20, 2026

ReputeX ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the ReputeX browser extension and Web3 security services.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

ReputeX is designed with strict privacy-by-default standards:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • No Private Keys or Credentials: ReputeX never requests, accesses, or stores private keys, seed phrases, or wallet credentials.
  • No Browsing History Tracking: We do not track, log, or sell your web browsing history across websites.

2. Data We Process

To provide real-time explainable Web3 risk scoring, ReputeX processes public on-chain information:

  • Public Wallet Addresses: When address scanning is active, public blockchain addresses (EVM, Bitcoin, Solana, ENS) visible on web pages are read locally to perform reputation analysis.
  • Local Browser Storage: We use local browser storage (chrome.storage.local) solely to save user preferences (such as enabling or disabling auto-scan) and temporarily cache reputation reports on your device to optimize performance.

3. Third-Party Web3 APIs & AI Services

To compute wallet scores and generate AI assessments, ReputeX communicates with encrypted external API endpoints:

  • Blockchain Explorers (Etherscan, Blockstream Esplora): Queries public on-chain transaction metrics and balances.
  • OpenRouter AI / Nvidia Nemotron: Synthesizes full context payloads into natural language security responses for Q&A queries.

All network communication occurs over secure HTTPS protocols.

4. Data Security

ReputeX adheres strictly to Manifest V3 security standards, using read-only DOM node traversal and isolated extension page permissions without string-based script injection.
